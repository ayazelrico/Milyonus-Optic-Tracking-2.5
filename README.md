# Milyonus Optic Tracking v3.0 — 90-Day Hustle Plan

[svg](https://github.com/ayazelrico/Milyonus-Optic-Tracking-2.5#milyonus-optic-tracking-v30--90-day-hustle-plan)

> **One measurable improvement. Every day. No random feature development.**

The purpose of this roadmap is neither modest nor merely technical: it is to elevate **Milyonus Optic Tracking** from the status of a competent technological demonstration into that of a dependable, interpretable, resilient, and product-grade optic intelligence engine.

MediaPipe shall remain the infrastructural substratum upon which the system rests; it is not, however, the ultimate source of our distinction. The enduring value of the platform shall reside within our **Proprietary Intelligence Layer** — a proprietary stratum of measurement models, temporal reasoning, behavioral interpretation, adaptive personalization, reliability mechanisms, and developer-facing capabilities through which the system shall acquire an identity unmistakably its own.

Our objective is therefore not to accumulate features indiscriminately, nor to mistake complexity for progress. Rather, each day shall be governed by a singular principle:

> **One measurable improvement. Every day.**

Every modification must possess a demonstrable purpose, every purpose a measurable consequence, and every measurable consequence a reproducible basis.

## Six-Phase Roadmap

| **Phase**                               | **Days** | **Strategic Objective**                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

| **Phase 1 — Measurement Foundation**    |     1–15 | Establish a trustworthy measurement substrate and a reproducible baseline through landmark jitter analysis, temporal smoothing, confidence calibration, and unified signal schemas.                                                           |

## Daily Operating Cycle — The Hustle Rule

[svg](https://github.com/ayazelrico/Milyonus-Optic-Tracking-2.5#daily-operating-cycle--the-hustle-rule)

Every working day shall proceed according to the same nine-stage discipline. No development shall be undertaken merely for the seduction of novelty; each intervention must answer to a demonstrable deficiency, instability, or opportunity for measurable refinement.

1. **Observe:** Identify a metric that is absent, noisy, unstable, ambiguous, or materially misleading.

2. **Diagnose:** Establish the present failure mode and determine a measurable baseline against which subsequent improvement may be judged.

3. **Hypothesize:** Articulate, with precision, what ought to improve, by how much where practicable, and according to which metric the efficacy of the proposed intervention shall be determined.

4. **Implement:** Introduce one narrowly circumscribed logical change. The intended expenditure of effort shall ordinarily remain between **30 minutes and 3 hours**.

5. **Test:** Whenever circumstances permit, subject the modification to observation through a real camera, supplemented by the relevant automated test suite.

6. **Measure:** Compare the resulting behavior against the preceding implementation by means of reproducible measurements rather than intuition or impression.

7. **Review:** Examine the modification for regressions in accuracy, stability, latency, resource consumption, interpretability, and backward compatibility.

8. **Commit:** Preserve each coherent logical alteration as a discrete commit, employing `feat`, `fix`, `perf`, or `refactor` according to the nature of the intervention.

9. **Record:** Complete the daily record with the objective, alteration performed, governing metric, observed result, regression status, and subsequent action.

> **Golden Rule:** If we cannot answer the question *“Which measurement does this feature improve?”*, then that feature shall not be written today.

## Günlük Çalışma Devri — Hustle Rule

[svg](https://github.com/ayazelrico/Milyonus-Optic-Tracking-2.5#g%C3%BCnl%C3%BCk-%C3%A7al%C4%B1%C5%9Fma-d%C3%B6ng%C3%BCs%C3%BC--hustle-rule)

Her çalışma günü, istisnasız olarak aynı dokuz merhaleden müteşekkil disiplinli bir amelî devir içerisinde cereyan edecektir.

Geliştirme faaliyeti, sırf yeni bir şey ortaya koymanın cazibesine kapılarak icra edilmeyecek; her müdahale, sistemde mevcut bir noksanlık, istikrarsızlık, yanıltıcılık yahut ölçülebilir bir tekâmül imkânına istinat edecektir.

1. **Observe:** Eksik, gürültülü, kararsız, muğlak veya yanıltıcı olan metriği tespit et.

2. **Diagnose:** Mevcut hata biçimini teşhis et ve müteakip iyileştirmenin mukayese edileceği ölçülebilir bir baseline tesis et.

3. **Hypothesize:** Teklif edilen müdahalenin hangi metriği, ne istikamette ve mümkün olduğu takdirde ne miktarda iyileştirmesi icap ettiğini açık ve kati surette beyan et.

4. **Implement:** Mahiyeti ve kapsamı mümkün mertebe mahdut, tek bir mantıksal değişiklik tatbik et. Hedeflenen emek müddeti ekseriyetle **30 dakika ile 3 saat** arasında tutulacaktır.

5. **Test:** Şartlar elverdiği nispette değişikliği hakiki bir kamera üzerinden müşahade et ve bunu ilgili otomatik testlerle teyit et.

6. **Measure:** Elde edilen yeni neticeyi, sezgi veya intiba yerine yeniden üretilebilir ölçümler vasıtasıyla evvelki implementasyonla mukayese et.

7. **Review:** Değişikliğin accuracy, stability, latency, kaynak sarfiyatı, yorumlanabilirlik ve backward compatibility cihetlerinden herhangi bir regression meydana getirip getirmediğini tetkik et.

8. **Commit:** Her müstakil mantıksal değişikliği ayrı bir commit olarak muhafaza et; değişikliğin mahiyetine göre `feat`, `fix`, `perf` veya `refactor` etiketlerinden münasip olanını kullan.

9. **Record:** Günlük kaydı objective, change, metric, result, regression status ve next action unsurlarını ihtiva edecek surette tamamla.

> **Altın Kaide:** *“Bu özellik hangi ölçümü geliştiriyor?”* sualine açık ve ölçülebilir bir cevap veremiyorsak, o gün o özelliğin kodunu yazmayız.
